# S23 · Playtest protocol and polish standards
**Milestone:** M2–M4 · **Size:** process

## Purpose
"Polished before friends see it" (P8) needs a definition, or polishing never ends and the ritual never starts.

## Solo playtests (you, M1–M3)
Play on your **real phone, with a real Messenger call running** (even a call with one friend or a second device). After each session, write down:
1. **Signs of manual tracking** (Balatro lesson): did you count tricks on your fingers, forget the contract, or squint at whose turn it was? → each one is a UI bug.
2. **Waiting:** any moment you were waiting on the app, not on a person → a pace bug.
3. **Effects too big:** any effect that got annoying the 10th time → turn it down.
4. **The best moment:** what felt great → protect it.

## Dress rehearsal (M4, you + 1–2 friends)
- A full start-to-end night, including the scheduler.
- Afterwards, three questions for each friend: *What was the best moment? What got in the way? Would you do this again next week?*

## Launch bar (all must be true)
- [ ] Zero rules bugs in the last 30 deals played.
- [ ] Zero accidental plays or bids in the last 30 deals.
- [ ] A reconnect works on every friend's actual phone.
- [ ] The M2 side-by-side check passed (effects on feels better and no slower).
- [ ] The scheduler has locked at least one real slot.
- [ ] You'd be proud to send the link.

## After launch
Keep a short "next session" list. Ship **at most one** small improvement between nights, so each night feels a bit new without anything breaking.
